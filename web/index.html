Today's call

01

Compute scarcity is the new pricing power.

Anthropic locked a 4-year, $1.8B, 300 MW lease on xAI's Colossus 1 (220k+ GPUs) and is publicly citing compute as their growth bottleneck. Alphabet raised 2026 capex to $180–190B; Meta reaffirmed $115–135B + a $27B Nebius/Louisiana JV.

A new FM provider without a credible compute story is already losing the room.

Touches: Anthropic, xAI, Google, Meta
02

DeepSeek's first external raise resets the cost narrative.

Reported $3–7B at a $50B valuation (5× in three weeks), with V4 Pro at $0.435 in / $0.87 out per 1M tokens and cache-hit prices cut to 1/10.

The "Chinese cost disruptor" thesis now has Western capital scale behind it — undercutting on price alone is no longer a defensible wedge.

Touches: DeepSeek · indirect: OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral
03

xAI is restructuring under fire.

Musk dissolved xAI as a standalone entity (folding into "SpaceXAI"), 11 of 12 cofounders gone, Grok DAU −12% MoM globally / −21% US, two-week deprecation notice for 8 legacy models drew developer backlash. The Anthropic lease is a $5B/yr ARR backstop, but xAI is now functionally a neocloud that also makes models.

Worth watching: how fast a model lab can be re-platformed under capital duress.

Touches: xAI, Anthropic
