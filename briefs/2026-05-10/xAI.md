# xAI — 2026-05-10

**Domain:** x.ai · **Relationship:** competitor · **Lens:** competitive_intel (PM, new FM provider)

## TL;DR
- xAI dissolved as standalone entity, folded into "SpaceXAI" on May 6 alongside a 4-year, ~$5B/yr lease of Colossus 1 to Anthropic ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/06/anthropic-spacex-data-center-capacity.html), [latent.space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-anthropic-spacexais-300mw5byr)).
- Grok 4.3 (April 30) hit the API at $1.25/$2.50 per M tokens with 1M context — ~40% input / ~58% output price cut vs Grok 4.20 ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/x-ai/grok-4.3), [datastudios.org](https://www.datastudios.org/post/grok-4-3-characteristics-pricing-benchmarks-context-window-api-access-and-what-changed-from-gr)).
- 8 legacy Grok models retire May 15 with ~2 weeks' notice; usage down 12% MoM globally and Grok now #5 behind Claude/Gemini/DeepSeek ([Apiyi](https://help.apiyi.com/en/grok-4-3-release-xai-api-model-retirement-en.html), [Yahoo Tech](https://tech.yahoo.com/ai/claude/articles/elon-musks-grok-takes-hit-050000178.html)).

## News & announcements
- May 6: Anthropic-SpaceX compute partnership announced; Anthropic gets all of Colossus 1 (220k+ Nvidia GPUs, 300MW) for 4 years at ~$5B/yr ([xAI](https://x.ai/news/anthropic-compute-partnership), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/06/anthropic-spacex-data-center-capacity.html)).
- May 6: Musk announces xAI dissolution; AI products consolidated under "SpaceXAI" sub-brand of SpaceX ([Republic World](https://www.republicworld.com/tech/musk-says-xai-will-be-dissolved-as-separate-company-ai-products-to-come-under-spacexai), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/technology/articles/xai-dead-long-live-spacexai-183140341.html)).
- May 6: API email to all users — eight legacy models (incl. grok-4, grok-4-fast, grok-3, grok-code-fast-1) retire May 15 12:00 PT ([Apiyi](https://help.apiyi.com/en/grok-4-3-release-xai-api-model-retirement-en.html)).

## Pricing & packaging changes
- Grok 4.3: $1.25 / $2.50 per 1M input/output tokens — vs Grok 4.20 at $2.00 / $6.00 (~40% / ~58% cut) ([OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/x-ai/grok-4.3)).
- Image input also $1.25/M; 1M context with no output cap repositions cost curve for agentic apps ([mem0.ai](https://mem0.ai/blog/xai-grok-api-pricing)).
- Enterprise: dedicated API capacity with guaranteed TPM now bookable; Grok Business holds at $30/seat/mo ([Releasebot](https://releasebot.io/updates/xai), [Threads](https://www.threads.com/@testingcatalog/post/DS7naucgr00/x-ai-introduced-grok-for-business-and-grok-enterprise-plans-grok-for-business)).

- Standing wins (older): DoD $200M "Grok for Government" contract (April), Shift4 customer-service partnership ([Big Hat Group](https://www.bighatgroup.com/blog/xai-weekly-2026-04-29/), [Payments Dive](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/shift4-taps-xai-for-customer-service/813580/)).
- No new enterprise logo announcements in last 48h.

## What this means for us
- The Anthropic-Colossus deal flips the script: xAI is now a compute landlord renting to a top-tier rival, which means Grok itself is less compute-constrained relative to the field — but also less differentiated as "the company with the biggest cluster." Our pitch should lean into model quality and developer experience, not raw compute narratives.
- Grok 4.3 at $1.25/$2.50 with 1M context is now a serious price floor for frontier-tier reasoning APIs — undercutting Claude Sonnet/GPT-class pricing. If our launch pricing assumed Grok 4 levels ($2/$6), re-baseline now; the May 15 deprecation chaos is also a real wedge to court burned Grok devs.
