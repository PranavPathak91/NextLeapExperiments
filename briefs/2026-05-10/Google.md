## What this means for us
- Google's distribution moat is widening fast — Cloud +63%, $460B backlog, and Adobe/Atlassian agents inside Gemini Enterprise. Competing on raw model quality alone won't move enterprise deals; we need a partnership/integration wedge before I/O on May 19 reshuffles attention again.
- The DeepMind unionization + Pentagon backlash is an opening for us with mission-sensitive enterprise buyers (EU public sector, healthcare, ESG-conscious accounts). Position now while Google is publicly entangled in defense-contract optics.
