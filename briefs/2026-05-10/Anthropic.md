## What this means for us
- Anthropic is not competing on per-token price — they're competing on **vertical workflows + distribution channels** (M365, Moody's, Goldman/Blackstone JV). A new FM provider needs a wedge that doesn't require this scale of partnerships: a horizontal capability gap (latency, context window pricing, on-prem) or a vertical Anthropic isn't lavishing attention on (manufacturing, healthcare ops, public sector).
- Compute scarcity is their visible weakness — 80x growth, peak-hour throttling only just removed, $1.8B emergency Akamai spend. Position our reliability/availability SLAs aggressively to enterprise buyers burned by Anthropic's April rate-limit reshuffles and Opus 4.7 regressions; the "Claude-lash" sentiment is a real wedge for the next 90 days.
