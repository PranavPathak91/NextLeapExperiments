## What this means for us
- Talent risk just inverted: DeepSeek will issue equity for the first time, funded by a state-backed war chest. Window to recruit their researchers narrows fast — move on any open req targeting their alumni in the next 4-6 weeks before the round closes.
- Pricing pressure intensifies on May 31 when the V4 Pro 75% promo ends — but they may extend or cut list price to defend share. Plan our list-price positioning assuming V4 Pro stays at promo levels (~$0.43 in / $0.87 out) rather than reverting; if we can't match within 3-5x, we need a clear non-price wedge (latency SLA, region, eval transparency).
