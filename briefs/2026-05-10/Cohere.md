## What this means for us
- Cohere is doubling down on "sovereign + regulated buyer" as the moat US labs can't easily copy — if our wedge is enterprise, we need a credible sovereign/data-residency story (deployment options, EU/CA hosting partner) before pitching financial-services or public-sector accounts.
- The North platform is shifting Cohere from API vendor to agentic workspace; selling raw model access into Cohere's installed base will get harder. Counter by leading with model quality + per-token economics where Command R+ at $2.50/$10 is the price ceiling we must undercut on similar quality tiers.
