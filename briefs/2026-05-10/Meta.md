# Meta — 2026-05-10

**Domain:** ai.meta.com · **Relationship:** competitor · **Lens:** competitive_intel (PM, new FM provider)

## TL;DR
- Five major publishers + Scott Turow filed a class action May 5 over Llama training data, citing 82TB of LibGen torrents personally approved by Zuckerberg. ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/05/nx-s1-5812623/scott-turow-meta-lawsuit), [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/meta-ai-mark-zuckerberg-copyright-infringement-lawsuit-publishers-scott-turow-1236738383/))
- May 20 layoff of ~8,000 (10% of HC) lands while Meta reaffirms $115–135B 2026 AI capex and continues hiring AI/ML roles. ([TNW](https://thenextweb.com/news/meta-layoffs-may-2026-ai-restructuring-thousands))
- Muse Spark (closed-weight, MSL-built) remains Meta's flagship; no new Llama release announced — open-weight cadence has stalled. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/08/meta-debuts-the-muse-spark-model-in-a-ground-up-overhaul-of-its-ai/))

## News & announcements
- May 5: Elsevier, Cengage, Hachette, Macmillan, McGraw Hill + Turow sued Meta in SDNY over Llama training data. ([Publishing Perspectives](https://publishingperspectives.com/2026/05/publishers-and-authors-sue-meta-alleging-massive-copyright-infringement-behind-its-llama-ai-service/))
- Complaint cites 82TB of pirated content and alleges Zuckerberg personally authorized LibGen use after killing licensing talks April 2023. ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/digital/news/meta-ai-mark-zuckerberg-copyright-infringement-lawsuit-publishers-scott-turow-1236738383/))
- Meta's response via PA director Nkechi Nneji: training is fair use; will fight aggressively. ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/meta-ai-lawsuit-copyright-scott-turow-publishers-llama/))

## Product / feature launches
- No new model launches in last 48h. Muse Spark (April 8) remains the live flagship — natively multimodal, "Contemplating" multi-agent reasoning mode, closed-weight via private API preview. ([Meta blog](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-msl/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/08/meta-debuts-the-muse-spark-model-in-a-ground-up-overhaul-of-its-ai/))
- Llama 4 Scout/Maverick (open-weight, MoE, multimodal) remains current open-weight tier; no Llama 5 timeline disclosed. ([ai.meta.com](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-4-multimodal-intelligence/))

## Hiring & org changes

- Business AI tools still free for SMBs; Zuckerberg flagged future paid tier. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/30/meta-says-its-business-ai-now-facilitates-10-million-conversations-a-week/))

## Customer wins / logos
- No material signal in the last 48h.

## What this means for us
- Meta's open-weight pause + lawsuit exposure opens a credibility gap: enterprises wary of Llama provenance are an addressable wedge for a clean-data foundation model — lead with training-data attestation in pitches.
- Closed-weight Muse Spark via private-preview API mirrors OpenAI/Anthropic packaging; Meta is no longer the "free open-weight floor" pinning down API pricing — we have more room on price/positioning than 12 months ago.
