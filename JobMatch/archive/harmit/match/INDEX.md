## Honest meta-gap (across all roles)

The single most binding gap on the entire shortlist is **YoE**. Five of the ten roles have floors at 4–5+ yrs vs the candidate's ~2.5. CV polish lifts probability within the recruiter's discretion band but never crosses a hard YoE screen. The two highest-fit roles (GroMo, Kikoff) are the two where YoE is *not* a problem — that's not a coincidence.
